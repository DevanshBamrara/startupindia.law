# StartupIndia.Law — "The Sanctuary"

*A Digital Temple for Founders — Office of Adv. Ashish Gupta*

StartupIndia.Law is an interactive digital sanctuary designed for startup founders seeking legal advisory, entity architecture, IP fortification, and regulatory guidance in India and across global jurisdictions.

---

## 🏛 Visual & Atmospheric Features

- **Global Sanctuary Aesthetics**: Aged lime plaster background (`#FAF7F2`) with fine paper noise texture overlay and soft natural lighting (`~4500K`).
- **Frame 1 (The Bell)**: Golden-ratio landing view with recessed stone query basin (*"Ask us how."*) and breathing inquiry reveal.
- **Frame 2 (The Corridor)**: Horizontal snap corridor with carved granite trust panels and synchronized tally mark indicators.
- **Frame 3 (The Sanctum)**: Recessed practice niches with interactive **Diya flame glow radial gradients**.
- **Frame 4 (The Gallery of Beings)**: Museum vitrines displaying invention artifacts with inline case study drawers.
- **Frame 5 (The Wisdom Pool)**: Dusk atmospheric transition with mechanical counter (`113,000+`) and scroll-triggered revelations.
- **Frame 6 (The Tree & The Seated One)**: Ancient Bodhi tree line drawing SVG, seated silhouette, and physical pamphlet element.
- **Frame 7 (The Offering & Disclaimer)**: Government schemes detail modals, incubators list, connection query basin, and comprehensive legal disclaimers.
